Our Story

Crafted with passion,
served with love.

Born from a love of slow mornings and good conversations, Aroma & Co. opened its doors in 2019 with one simple belief — that great coffee should feel like a warm hug.

We source single-origin beans directly from estates across Coorg and Chikmagalur, roasted in-house every morning so every cup you get is as fresh as it gets.
